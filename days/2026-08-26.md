## Why the afternoon is empty

A sharp day with nothing booked is exactly when a plan gets overstuffed, because
the hours look free and the energy feels infinite at 9am. The afternoon is left
open on purpose: the morning's processing will surface real work, and that work
deserves the good hours — not a slot assigned before anyone knew what it was.

If the morning runs long, it runs long. That is the buffer functioning, not the
plan failing.
